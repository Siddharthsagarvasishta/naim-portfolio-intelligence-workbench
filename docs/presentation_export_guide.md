# Presentation Export Guide

## Purpose

Presentation export converts an approved evidence snapshot into an editable executive pack. Titles, charts, tables and commentary remain editable where practical. The export is a point-in-time report, not a live analytical model.

## Standard pack

Title/scope, executive summary, portfolio scorecard, KPI movement, exact root-cause bridge, vintage evidence, strategy trade-off, partner, vendor, membership, forecast/stress, alerts/investigations, quality/methodology and metric appendix.

## Required footer and notes

Every slide shows reporting period, filter scope, refresh time, metric version and synthetic-data disclaimer. Speaker notes include key message, supporting evidence, caveat, likely management question, recommended response and a `[Sources]` block.

## Integrity checks

- All numbers trace to the evidence snapshot.
- Current/prior changes and basis points use full precision.
- Mix plus performance equals observed movement.
- Scenario deltas reconcile to baseline.
- Charts and table labels match metric units.
- Quality warnings and non-causal caveats are visible.
- No unsupported numeric claim appears in commentary.

## Approval

Users may choose period, workspace, basket, template, slides and commentary detail. Regeneration creates a new artifact version; it never overwrites an approved pack without an audit event.
