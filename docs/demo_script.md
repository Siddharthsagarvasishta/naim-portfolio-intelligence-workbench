# Demo Scripts

All figures in the supplied artifacts are synthetic and trace to `exports/validation/evidence_snapshot.json`.

## 60-second story

**0–10 seconds — movement.** “nAIM starts with a live governed evidence snapshot. At the calculated August 2025 peak, annualized net loss rate is 6.69%, up 311.4 basis points month on month; the data-quality gate passed.”

**10–25 seconds — diagnosis.** “The exact bridge attributes 4.4 basis points to mix and 307.0 to within-segment performance. Affiliate is the largest acquisition-channel contribution; its share can exceed 100% because offsetting segments reduce the net movement.”

**25–38 seconds — corroboration.** “Maturity-aligned 2025 Q2–Q3 Affiliate vintages deteriorate at months-on-book 4–8. The result is associative, so nAIM proposes an investigation, not a blanket tightening.”

**38–50 seconds — trade-off.** “Challenger B has 4.69 fraud basis points versus 15.86 for Champion A, but a 89.75% false-positive rate. The sample-ratio test fails, so nAIM labels the broader comparison investigatory rather than causal.”

**50–60 seconds — governed handoff.** “The same metric versions and evidence feed Excel, Power BI, Tableau, SAS and editable PowerPoint outputs. Baskets and workspaces preserve the question, population, configuration and audit trail.”

## Five-minute walkthrough

1. **Command centre (35 sec):** establish the calculated peak reporting period, scope, refresh/run ID and quality status. Show 513,923 account-month observations and loss, delinquency, fraud, review, friction and expected profit together.
2. **Root cause (40 sec):** open the 311.4 bps bridge. Demonstrate 4.4 bps of mix plus 307.0 bps of within-segment performance and the effectively zero residual. Explain why a contribution share may exceed 100% when other segments offset the primary driver.
3. **Vintage (30 sec):** compare Affiliate cohorts at equal maturity; disclose cohort size and immature cells.
4. **Strategy impact (40 sec):** compare Champion A and Challenger B. Fraud improves, but operational load and customer harm increase. Review randomization/balance checks before causal language.
5. **Partner impact (25 sec):** show Atlas Digital Acquisition’s calculated negative monthly contribution and compare it with defensible peers rather than the full population.
6. **Vendor impact (25 sec):** show vendor quality, cost and contract-capacity utilization. Explain why substitution is a scenario, not an automated recommendation.
7. **Membership economics (20 sec):** connect engagement, benefit cost, loss and contribution; the live snapshot shows Gold near break-even while Platinum contributes positively.
8. **Mild downturn (30 sec):** the 12-month baseline expected profit is about 2.96m synthetic currency units versus 2.07m under the conditional mild scenario; label the roughly −0.89m result a scenario estimate anchored to the latest available month.
9. **Investigation workflow (25 sec):** open the loss-concentration alert, assign an owner, review evidence and show auditable state transitions.
10. **Controlled commentary (25 sec):** generate a draft from the evidence object; numerical verifier rejects unsupported claims and the user remains accountable.
11. **Enterprise outputs (25 sec):** show reconciled Excel and PowerPoint plus Power BI/Tableau/SAS source packages.

Close with: “nAIM is not another dashboard. It is the reusable, governed analytical layer that preserves definitions, populations, comparisons, evidence and decision history.”
