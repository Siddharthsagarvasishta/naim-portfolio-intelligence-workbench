# Partner Analytics Methodology

## Scope and grain

Partner performance is measured at partner–month with optional product, region and membership-tier dimensions. Contract terms are joined by effective date. Attributed outcomes use an explicit attribution rule and must not be double-counted across acquisition, transaction and benefit relationships.

## Economics

`Partner contribution = partner revenue + funded benefit value − partner fees − rebates − incentives − benefit cost share − servicing cost − attributed credit/fraud/dispute cost`

Risk-adjusted contribution additionally charges configured concentration, volatility or capital proxies. Every component remains visible; no single blended score replaces the economics.

## Performance and risk

Review acquisition, active-account contribution, transaction value, loss, fraud, complaints, attrition, SLA, contract performance and concentration. Rates are ratios of summed governed numerators/denominators. Contract minimums, caps, bonuses and penalties are versioned assumptions.

## Peer and rating use

Compare partners with analogues matched on category, scale, region, tenure and contractual structure. Weak matches trigger a confidence warning. Ratings expose component values, normalization, weights and methodology version. Internal grades are not agency ratings.

## Decisions

Outputs support renewal review, remediation, targeted growth, pricing analysis and monitored substitution scenarios. They do not automate contractual decisions. Legal, relationship and operational constraints remain human inputs.
