# Interview Talking Points

## Why I built it

Recurring portfolio reviews often reproduce metric logic and segmentation in disconnected spreadsheets or dashboards. nAIM demonstrates how a governed analytical layer can make risk, profitability and operating trade-offs repeatable across tools.

## Architecture

Raw data is immutable; validation gates a canonical model; conformed facts feed a versioned metric registry; analytical services produce evidence contracts; the API, application and exports consume identical evidence. This separates calculation from presentation.

## Metric governance

Every rate exposes numerator, denominator, exclusions, aggregation behaviour, minimum sample, owner and version. Rate rollups are ratio-of-sums. Configuration changes have impact previews and approval history.

## Deterioration story

The synthetic story combines faster Affiliate acquisition, weaker recent-vintage performance and mild macro pressure. Exact decomposition separates mix from within-segment deterioration. The response is targeted investigation, not indiscriminate tightening.

## Correlation versus causality

Contribution analysis and SHAP identify associations. Causal language is reserved for valid randomized comparisons or defensible quasi-experimental designs with overlap and sensitivity checks. The interface labels this explicitly.

## Champion–challenger validity

I check sample-ratio mismatch, baseline standardized differences, contamination, minimum samples, multiple outcomes and practical guardrails. Fraud improvement alone is insufficient if review cost, false positives, friction or expected profit worsen.

## Model risk

Models and analytical templates have owners, versions, validation evidence, thresholds, drift monitoring, change triggers and retirement criteria. Deterministic formulas are governed components too.

## Restricted GenAI

The LLM never calculates KPIs. It receives a bounded evidence object, may only produce draft narrative, and passes a numerical-claim verifier. Provider, model, prompt version and timestamps are auditable.

## Interoperability

Power BI, Tableau, Excel, SAS and PowerPoint are consumption channels. Their measures reference the central metric dictionary and selected-snapshot reconciliation totals. No fabricated binary artifacts are claimed.

## Limits and next step

The data and story are synthetic; observational results remain non-causal; local storage/auth are demo-grade. The strongest next enhancement is institution-grade onboarding with contract tests against a real canonical schema and independent validation of the first production metric pack.
