# Operations Capacity Methodology

## Measures

Demand, effective staffed capacity, cases completed, pending inventory, average and 90th-percentile handling time, first-time-right rate, rework, abandonment, SLA breaches, downtime and unit cost.

`Capacity utilization = eligible workload / effective available capacity`

Effective capacity accounts for staffing, productive time, skills, planned downtime and shrinkage. Values above 100% are permitted and indicate backlog risk.

## Forecast

Use recent arrivals, handling time distribution, staffing calendar and known events. Compare with a seasonal naive baseline. Scenario ranges change volume, handling time, absenteeism, outage or strategy review rate. State whether a queueing approximation or deterministic flow model is used.

## Quality trade-off

Higher throughput is not favourable if errors, rework, false positives or complaints rise. Capacity recommendations jointly assess service, quality, risk and cost.

## Controls

Disclose the workload unit, time window, censoring, reopened cases, cross-skilled resources and outsourced capacity. Avoid utilization averages across incompatible services. A proposed reallocation requires eligibility, transition and control-equivalence checks.
