# Customer and Membership Methodology

## Unit of analysis

Customer views deduplicate accounts; account views retain product-level exposures. Membership results use customer/tier/month grain and effective-dated tier definitions. Cross-product holding and benefit use require a stable synthetic customer key.

## Value and risk

Membership contribution equals fee and transaction/balance/partner revenue less benefit, servicing, funding, credit, fraud and friction costs. Risk-adjusted member value uses transparent assumptions and a declared horizon; it is not presented as accounting profit.

## Lifecycle

Track acquisition, activation, engagement, benefit utilization, upgrade, downgrade, retention, attrition and cross-product holding. Transition matrices reconcile origin states to destination states. Upgrades/downgrades are measured only among eligible beginning members.

## Segmentation

Business-rule segments are primary and auditable. K-means or tree surrogates are optional exploratory tools; stability, scaling, sample sufficiency and interpretability are assessed. Sensitive attributes are excluded unless expressly approved for fairness testing.

## Caveats

Benefit profitability depends on attribution, breakage, delayed settlement and funding assumptions. High engagement can coexist with weak contribution; low loss alone does not imply a valuable proposition.
