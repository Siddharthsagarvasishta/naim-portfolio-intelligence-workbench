# Instant Demo Guide

The Instant Demo presents the synthetic portfolio story in 30–60 seconds with no configuration.

## Sequence

1. **Movement:** annualized loss rate increases; materiality and quality status are visible.
2. **Concentrated driver:** exact mix/performance bridge and Affiliate contribution.
3. **Vintage evidence:** newer Affiliate cohorts at months-on-book 4–8.
4. **Strategy trade-off:** lower fraud versus review, false positives, friction and capacity.
5. **Action:** targeted investigation rather than portfolio-wide tightening.
6. **Handoff:** same evidence in governed enterprise export packages.

## Presenter controls

Run/pause/restart; concise/analyst explanation; open source evidence; and jump to the full module. The demo does not mutate configuration or investigations.

## Integrity

All figures come from the selected evidence snapshot; the run ID, reporting month, metric version, refresh time and synthetic disclaimer remain visible. If quality is blocked or evidence does not reconcile, the demo shows the failure rather than a cached success story.
