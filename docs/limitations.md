# Limitations

- All data, names, contracts, findings and currency values are synthetic and institution-neutral.
- Demo findings illustrate a seeded analytical story; they are not customer, partner, vendor or market facts.
- The local analytical store and development authentication pattern are not a production control environment.
- Observational root-cause, peer, SHAP and strategy views identify associations unless a valid randomized design supports causal interpretation.
- Annualized monthly rates are presentation measures, not forecasts.
- Scenario outputs are conditional estimates and are not regulatory stress-test results.
- Small samples, immature vintages and weak peer matches can make estimates unstable; the UI should suppress or warn.
- Rating grades are internal synthetic categories, not agency ratings.
- Power BI, Tableau, SAS, VBA and VBScript packages are source/configuration examples. No `.pbix`, Hyper extract or executed SAS result is claimed.
- Macro-enabled code is optional and should be reviewed, signed and distributed under enterprise policy.
- PowerPoint and Excel exports are point-in-time evidence snapshots; refresh metadata and reconciliation are required before reuse.
- Commentary is deterministic, local and draft-only; no external LLM provider is implemented.
- Alert cooldown, durable cross-run deduplication, parent-child suppression,
  workflow persistence and SLA enforcement require a state/history service and
  are not implemented by the in-memory demonstration engine.
- Network, capacity and substitution scenarios simplify real contractual, legal and operational dependencies.
- Performance, accessibility, security and browser coverage require institution-specific validation before deployment.
