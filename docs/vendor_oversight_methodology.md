# Vendor Oversight Methodology

## Scope

Vendor analytics combine service volume, cycle time, first-time-right quality, rework, errors, incidents, downtime, complaints, cost, capacity, concentration, contract status and control ratings at vendor–service–month grain.

## Core calculations

- Unit cost = total governed vendor cost / completed eligible volume.
- SLA breach rate = breached eligible cases or periods / eligible cases or periods.
- Capacity utilization = demand / effective approved capacity.
- Quality score uses versioned target-based or percentile components; missing values are penalized or excluded only as defined.
- Residual risk combines inherent risk and verified control effectiveness without averaging incompatible ordinal scales.

## Comparisons

Use matched service type, criticality, region, volume and delivery model. Disclose denominator, seasonality, material contract changes and weak peers. Cost comparisons must adjust for service mix and quality.

## Capacity and substitution

Queue or workload projections state assumptions about arrival rate, handling time, staffing and service levels. A substitution scenario considers spare capacity, transition delay, concentration, switching cost, fourth-party dependencies and control equivalence. It is not an automatic recommendation.

## Governance

Critical vendors require named owners, continuity evidence, security/operational ratings, exit-readiness assessment and change approval. Findings can open investigations and control recommendations with accountable owners and due dates.
